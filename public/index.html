Privacy & recovery

This is a small, private coordination app, not a clinical record or medication administration system. Only share someone's health information with their permission. Do not use it for dosing decisions or urgent treatment instructions.

Data is stored on the app's persistent Railway volume in the US West region. Medical records and medication tracking are not enabled in this release. Your owner can export roster data for safekeeping; downloads contain private information.
